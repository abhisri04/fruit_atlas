# Fruit Atlas finalization decisions

The active catalogue has **115 fruits**, **54 nested varieties**, and **160 recorded fruit-State/UT links**. This is an editorial freeze for identity and image filenames; source review remains open for 16 legacy fruits. No photographs are bundled.

The original 110 fruit IDs and the ten legacy redirects remain unchanged. FR121-FR125 were added as approved. No retired ID was reused. The existing completion and Saved keys remain unchanged.

## Decisions for all 61 original `legacy_review_needed` records

KEEP means an authoritative source now directly supports the Indian crop, use, or occurrence and the record is `source_verified`. NEEDS MORE REVIEW remains `legacy_review_needed`; those records stay active and retain their IDs. No fruit was retired.

## Taxonomy and photo identity

- FR048 remains a separate sweet-lime consumer record. Its name now uses the hybrid sign, *Citrus × limettioides*; [Kew treats that name as a synonym of lemon](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A771957-1). It stays in review, and a lemon photograph must not be substituted for sweet lime.
- FR099 now uses Kew's accepted name [*Diospyros nigra*](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A322764-1); *D. digyna* is the earlier synonym. The ID and image path did not change.
- FR002 (*Musa* spp.), FR026 (*Selenicereus* spp.), FR030 (*Pyrus* spp.), and FR036 (*Morus* spp.) are intentionally group-level records. FR029 covers *Prunus domestica* and *P. salicina*; FR032 covers *P. avium* and *P. cerasus*. A future photo should identify and depict a representative member of the stated group, with the photographed taxon retained in the manifest notes.
- FR054 *Syzygium aqueum*, FR055 *S. jambos*, FR056 *S. malaccense*, and FR110 *S. samarangense* are separate species. Record the photographed species, as common names vary by region.
- FR073 Bakul Fruit has documented edible-fruit use. FR095 Kadam Fruit also has a documented edible fruit. FR098 White Sapote is recorded by BSI as cultivated in northern India; FR099 and FR100 have botanical or horticultural source support. None is recommended for retirement on the reviewed evidence.
- Blueberry, Ceylon olive/Jalpai, quince, and Satkara/Hatkora remain review candidates without canonical IDs. [Kew currently treats *Citrus macroptera* as a synonym of FR083's *C. hystrix*](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/771970-1); do not create a Satkara record from its name alone.

## State links and variety architecture

The 68 new fruit-State/UT links each carry `state_id`, `status`, `evidence`, and `data_year` in the existing nested structure. Evidence URLs are also in `sourceRefs`. Links were added only for source-named places; a missing link is not evidence that a fruit is absent from a state. Existing 92 links were retained. Data years marked `not stated` are explicitly unknown, not inferred from retrieval date.

All 54 existing varieties and their nested parent fruits are unchanged. Sonaka remains a grape entry but should be labelled as a Thompson Seedless clone in later variety work. The separate [variety backlog](fruit_variety_backlog.md) lists candidates without adding them.
