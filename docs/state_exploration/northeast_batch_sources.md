# Northeast State Exploration research ledger

Research checked 17 September 2026. Government and official tourism sources were reviewed before runtime files were written. Descriptions are concise original summaries, not source quotations. Sources are documentation only; runtime schemas have no source fields.

## Administrative reconciliation

- Assam: 35 districts. The current government dashboard and Sribhumi portal take precedence over older lists using Karimganj. Karimganj remains a search alias.
- Arunachal Pradesh: 28 districts, including Bichom, Keyi Panyor and Itanagar Capital Complex. The state portal and investment district map take precedence over the grievance directory still listing 26. The separate Capital Complex portal confirms that administrative unit.
- Meghalaya: 12 districts, including Eastern West Khasi Hills.
- Nagaland: 17 districts, including Meluri. The state announcement and tourism district directory agree.
- Manipur: 16 districts, from the current NIC profile. The tourism destination page contains older administrative text; it is used only for attractions, never for district counts.

## Canonical choices

- Dzüko/Dzukou Valley appears once, under Nagaland; its description and keywords acknowledge Manipur. It is not recreated as a separate trek or a Manipur entity.
- Existing Momos, Thukpa, Butter Tea, Losar, generic carpet weaving and generic wood carving were omitted rather than duplicated.
- Regional named crafts and dishes represent distinct traditions; they are not aliases of existing generic records. Festival celebrations and performance forms are distinct experiences. Meghalaya dance festivals are listed only as festivals, not duplicated as Music & Dance entries.
- Drinks contain one documented non-alcoholic regional tea per state. Alcoholic rice drinks are excluded. Tea estate visits and drinking regional tea are distinct activities.
- Fruits have no static members or new Experience records. Membership and progress continue to use the canonical Fruit Atlas.
- No opening hours, ticket prices, guaranteed sightings, fixed festival dates or current travel-access promises are embedded in the data.
