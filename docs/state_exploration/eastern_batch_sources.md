# Eastern State Exploration batch: research ledger

Research date: 17 September 2026. This document is not a Flutter asset.

These are curated catalogues, not exhaustive inventories of each state's heritage. Descriptions were written for this repository rather than copied from source text. Shared dishes and traditions denote association with a state, not exclusive origin. Activity entries are editorial suggestions based on the documented places and crafts; they do not assert a particular operator, opening time, or guaranteed access.

The schema follows the populated Goa collections and experience records. All three travel collections belong to `things_to_do`. The underlying `place` experience type retains its established `Places` section label. Fruits have no explicit member IDs and continue to use the canonical Fruit Atlas relationships.

## Bihar

- District baseline: 38. [Bihar district directory](https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/en/districts), [state statistics](https://statedata.bihar.gov.in/). The [current Gaya Ji district website](https://gaya.nic.in/) supports the display name Gaya Ji; `Gaya` is retained as a search keyword.
- Heritage, arts, festivals and performance traditions: [Ministry of Tourism state overview](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/bihar).
- Heritage destinations, crafts and cultural events: [Bihar Tourism destination and cultural-topic catalogue](https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/content/dam/bihar-tourism/catalogue/topic-for-content-writing-contest.pdf).
- Heritage and visitor experiences: [Bihar Tourism's Blissful Bihar guide](https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/content/dam/bihar-tourism/e_brochures/blissful-bihar/blissful-bihar-brochure-eng-final.pdf).
- Nature: [Bihar Tourism](https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/) and [Ministry of Tourism perspective plan](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2020-04/Bihar.pdf). The older plan is used only for established sanctuary identities, not current opening arrangements or district boundaries.
- Dishes: [15 local dishes in Patna](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/bihar/patna/15-must-have-local-dishes-in-patna), [Bihar cuisine overview](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/iprdwebsite/Cuisineofbihar).
- Sattu drink: [Press Information Bureau regional drinks discussion](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2267168&lang=2&reg=3).
- Bel sharbat regional association: [NDTV Food regional drinks article](https://food.ndtv.com/food-drinks/7-lesser-known-regional-drinks-of-india-that-are-cooling-hydrating-and-mouth-watering-8997672). This secondary source supplements the government culinary sources; no health claims are carried into the data.
- Fairs: [Bihar government festival overview](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/bihartourism/biharfestivals).

## Sikkim

The former four-district structure is not used.
