# Fruit Atlas variety backlog

The active catalogue retains its 54 existing varieties. These are research
candidates for a later, separate expansion. Verify the cultivar identity,
parent fruit, Indian cultivation, preferred spelling, and any synonym or clone
relationship before assigning a variety ID. Do not use a fruit ID for a
variety.
